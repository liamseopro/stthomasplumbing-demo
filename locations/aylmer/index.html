Aylmer Plumbing Questions

Do you install water softeners in Aylmer?

Yes. Aylmer's water is among the hardest in Elgin County, and we size and install softeners tuned to real household demand so they regenerate efficiently.

Can you replace a rented water heater in Aylmer?

Yes. We swap rented tanks for owned, high-efficiency tank or tankless units across Aylmer, staged so the home is without hot water for hours, not days.

Do you offer emergency plumbing in Aylmer?

Yes. We take emergency calls across Aylmer and Elgin for burst pipes, backups, floods, and no-water situations, with a local crew that reaches you fast.

Do you repipe older Aylmer homes?

Yes. We replace galvanized and polybutylene piping in older Aylmer homes with clean PEX or copper, filed with the local authority and inspected.

Can you install a sump pump in my Aylmer basement?

Yes. We size sump pumps, battery backups, and backwater valves so Aylmer basements stay dry through spring thaw and heavy storms.
