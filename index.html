FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions St. Thomas homeowners ask us most.

Are you licensed plumbers?

Yes. We are licensed plumbers serving St. Thomas and Elgin County, and every job that requires one is filed with the local authority and inspected, so the work is legal, insurable, and documented.

Do you offer emergency plumbing service in St. Thomas?

Yes. We take emergency calls across St. Thomas and the surrounding Elgin area for floods, burst pipes, sewage backups, and no-water situations, with a local crew that reaches you fast.

Should I replace my galvanized pipes?

If you have an older St. Thomas home with galvanized steel lines, yes. Discoloured water, low pressure, and repeat pinhole leaks are the usual signs. We repipe in PEX or copper and file the permit.

Tank or tankless water heater for my St. Thomas home?

It depends on household size, hot-water demand, and whether you want to ditch the rented tank. We size both options honestly and quote them straight so you can choose on facts, not a sales pitch.

How do I know if I have a hidden leak?

A high water bill with no obvious cause, a damp spot, warm flooring, or the sound of running water when nothing is on are all signs. We use acoustic and thermal detection to pinpoint it before we open walls.

Does Elgin County water really need a softener?

Elgin draws some of the hardest water in southern Ontario, and most St. Thomas homes benefit from a softener. It protects the water heater, the fixtures, and your skin, and we size it to real demand.

How much does a plumbing job cost?

We offer free, no-obligation quotes, and we explain all pricing clearly before any work starts. Whether it is a toilet swap or a full repipe, there are no surprises.

Do you serve areas outside of St. Thomas?

Yes. We serve St. Thomas and the surrounding Elgin and southwest London communities including Aylmer, Belmont, Port Stanley, and parts of London.
